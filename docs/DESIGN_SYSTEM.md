# SURTIDO - premium product system

Direction approved in the user's steering: premium startup, precise and purposeful. Apply the design-system skill to tokens, consistent components, accessible states and meaningful numbers.

Colors: ink #161B22; canvas #F7F8FA; white #FFFFFF; cobalt #3455DB; border #E3E6EB; secondary ink #657080; success background #E0F5EB with ink #16593B; warning background #FFF3E1 with ink #855115. No gradients or purple. Shadows only for dialogs. Small 8-12px corner radii; no indiscriminate pills.

Type: local system sans stack; a distinctive bold lowercase wordmark and tight heading tracking; tabular numbers. Hero 50-64px desktop / 34px mobile; body 14-16px; metadata 12px minimum in the app. Generous 8px spacing scale. Native-looking custom inline vector icon set, no emojis, product photos or AI sparkles.

Main layout: narrow charcoal rail with visible short labels, 76px topbar, workspace max-width 1500px. A task heading, a three-part financial strip and a two-column work area with the decision summary. At 1100px collapse auxiliary chart; at 850px stack the summary; at 600px use bottom/compact navigation and product rows become cards. No horizontal page overflow at 390px.

Components: buttons (primary/secondary/quiet, disabled/busy/focus), labeled currency inputs, product table/card, quantity control, data-source chip, warning row, review dialog, receipt confirmation and explain panel. Every actionable component has a 44px target and clear keyboard focus; dialogs trap focus and Escape closes without applying changes. Use live regions for progress and errors, not repeated whole-page announcements.

Numbers always come from domain code. The generated mockup's numbers and chart placement are illustrative. Show no analytics the case cannot support. The three weekly demand points are explicitly the case observations, never a live-business metric.
